## Problem Title

**SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase consumer prices.**

**Problem Creator's Organization:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

**Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## 1. Proposed Solution

### FarmLink AI – Direct Farm-to-Buyer Marketplace

FarmLink AI is a digital agricultural marketplace that connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers and bulk buyers directly, reducing unnecessary intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain.

Farmers can upload details about their available products, quantity, quality, expected price and location. Consumers and bulk buyers can browse products, compare prices and place orders directly with farmers.

The platform also includes an integrated logistics system that connects farmers with transport providers to arrange product collection and delivery.

**How it addresses the problem:**

- Eliminates unnecessary trading intermediaries by enabling direct buyer-farmer transactions.
- Provides transparent pricing so farmers can make informed selling decisions.
- Allows multiple farmers to combine orders for efficient transportation.
- Connects consumers with fresh agricultural products at competitive prices.

**Innovation and Uniqueness:**

- AI-based demand forecasting to predict the future demand for agricultural products.
- Smart route optimization to reduce transportation time and costs.
- Local-language support for farmers.
- Real-time order and delivery tracking.
- Transparent price comparison between different buyers and markets.

### Methodology and Implementation

**Step 1: User Registration**

Farmers, FPOs, consumers, bulk buyers and transport providers register on the platform with their respective details.

**Step 2: Product Listing**

Farmers upload their agricultural products, including product name, available quantity, quality, price and location.

**Step 3: Direct Marketplace**

Consumers and bulk buyers browse available products, compare prices and place orders directly with farmers.

**Step 4: AI Demand Forecasting**

The AI system analyses historical orders, market prices and seasonal demand to predict the demand for different agricultural products.

**Step 5: Smart Logistics**

The system groups nearby orders and uses route optimization to suggest efficient transportation routes, helping reduce delivery costs and delays.

**Step 6: Order Tracking and Payment**

Buyers can track their orders, receive notifications and complete payments through supported digital payment methods.

### Working Flow

Farmer Registration → Product Listing → Buyer Selection → Order Placement → AI Demand Analysis → Logistics and Route Optimization → Product Delivery → Payment Confirmation.

## 3. Feasibility and Viability

### Feasibility of the Idea

The proposed solution can be developed using existing web technologies, cloud platforms, machine learning libraries and mapping services.

It can initially be implemented as a web application and later expanded into a mobile application.

A pilot implementation can begin with selected farmers, FPOs and local consumers in one agricultural region.

### Potential Challenges and Risks

1. Limited digital literacy among some farmers.
2. Difficulty in maintaining product quality during transportation.
3. Fluctuations in agricultural prices and product availability.
4. Transportation expenses and delivery delays.
5. Building trust between farmers and new buyers.

### Strategies to Overcome Challenges

- Provide a simple interface with local-language support.
- Include farmer training and assistance through FPOs.
- Introduce product quality verification and buyer feedback.
- Use AI-based route optimization to reduce transportation expenses.
- Maintain transparent pricing, order records and secure payment systems.
